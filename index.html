About

A systems engineer who builds AI, and an AI researcher who ships production-grade code.

I'm pursuing my MS in Computer Science at UC San Diego (GPA 3.8), where I split my time between two roles: Software Developer at Relevance Labs — building LLM-powered insurance agents with LangGraph and RAG — and Graduate Researcher at CAIDA, SDSC, where I re-architected a network packet analysis engine from legacy C to concurrent Go, hitting 1M+ packets per second.

My engineering foundation is in backend systems. At Comviva Technologies I owned carrier-grade Java microservices for a CPaaS platform sustaining 5,000+ SMS/sec, cut API latency by 30% with a Redis routing cache, and eliminated production race conditions across the Voice Processor pipeline. I'm at home writing kernel-adjacent Go, orchestrating Kubernetes deployments, or fine-tuning a BioBERT model on clinical datasets.

I'm drawn to problems that sit at the intersection of infrastructure and intelligence — where getting the engineering right is half the problem, and the other half is making the model actually useful.
